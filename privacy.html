The game stores progress, scores, settings, and unlocked content locally on your device. This information is used to maintain your game progress and preferences.

If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP addresses, and advertising interaction data for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, personalization.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

We plan to introduce optional Google Play Games sign-in, leaderboards, and cloud-based game progress synchronization in a future update.

These features are not described as active until they are released. This policy will be updated before they become available.

We do not sell personal information directly. Third-party service providers, including Google, may process information according to their own privacy policies when their services are enabled.

Locally stored game progress can generally be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game, subject to device backup and restoration settings.

For privacy questions or deletion requests relating to information controlled by Aethoryn Studios, please contact us using the address below.

7. Children's Privacy

The Perfect Gap is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children.
